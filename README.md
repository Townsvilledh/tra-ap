# Tra-ap — Say it. Hear it. 🎧

An **offline-first, open-source music studio for your phone**.
One single HTML file. No server, no account, no downloads, no samples to
license — every sound is synthesised live in your browser from original code.

Built for a phone in landscape (Samsung A12 and friends), designed to be a
real studio, not a toy — and deliberately **not** a clone of FL Studio or
Logic. See [SPEC.md](SPEC.md) for what it is, what it isn't, and why.

## Try it
- **Live:** open `index.html` from GitHub Pages (link in the repo header), or
- **Offline:** download `dist/tra-ap.html`, open it in any mobile/desktop
  browser. It works with no connection at all. Add it to your home screen
  and it behaves like an app.

## What's inside
- **Flow** — 64-bar scene playlist. Scenes are 1, 2 or 4 bars; double-tap a
  clip to edit it. Time counter with a 3:00 marker.
- **Rack** — step sequencer for 10 channels: kick, snare, hat, clap, shaker,
  amapiano log drum, bass, piano, marimba, pad.
- **Keys** — piano roll: tap to write, drag to move, drag the tail to
  resize, double-tap to change velocity.
- **Mix** — per-channel volume, pan, 3-band EQ, delay & reverb sends,
  mute/solo, master compressor.
- **Create** — the Tra-ap difference:
  - *Sound design by words* — "punchy kick", "dark log drum", "soft marimba"
  - *Sentence to song* — "sad piano in Am at 98 with soft drums" builds a scene
  - *Idea Cards* — Use / Blend / Dismiss generated grooves in 6 flavours
    (Trap, Afrobeats, Amapiano, Drill, Boom bap, Afro-soul)
  - *Listen (beta)* — hum a melody or beatbox; Tra-ap transcribes it
- **Sound Check** — solo-tests every channel and tells you *why* one is
  silent. Built to kill the classic "only the kick plays" bug.
- **Files** — projects save as `.traap.json` (+ autosave in the browser);
  import MIDI and audio samples; export **MIDI** and **WAV**.

## Honest limits
No VST plugins on mobile browsers. No .flp / Logic project read-write —
MIDI + WAV are the bridge. No automation lanes or timeline audio clips yet
(samples play as instruments). Full list and roadmap in [SPEC.md](SPEC.md).
